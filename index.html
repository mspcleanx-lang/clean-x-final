Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to supply our own cleaning equipment and chemicals?

No. Our crews arrive with professional scrubbers, cloths, vacuums, and eco-friendly disinfectants. If your facility requires specific approved solutions, just note it on WhatsApp.

How do you handle unoccupied or vacant properties?

We routinely clean vacant houses and newly constructed flats. We remove dust, stickers, paint droplets, and bathroom scaling. We can coordinate key pickups or video confirmations if you are away.

How is the travel fee calculated?

Any location within 3 km of our center is completely free. Above 3 km, we charge Rs 50 for each additional kilometer once per dispatch.

When is payment made?

No upfront deposit is required. Payment is made right after you or your site supervisor inspect the finished work.
